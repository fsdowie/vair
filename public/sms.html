VAiR text messages

VAiR (vaireferee.com) is an AI assistant that answers soccer referees' questions about the IFAB Laws of the Game. This page explains the text messages VAiR sends and how people agree to receive them.

Who gets texts

VAiR sends text messages only to people who asked to try VAiR during its beta test. Referees give their mobile number directly to the VAiR team, in person or in a message, and agree at that time to receive a text inviting them to the beta. VAiR never buys, rents or collects phone numbers from lists or third parties.

How consent is given (verbal opt-in)

Consent is given verbally, in person or on a call, or in writing in a direct message. Before taking a number, the VAiR team asks this question, and texts only people who answer yes:

Consent script
"Can VAiR send you a text with a link to join the VAiR beta? It's at most 3 messages. Message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help."

The VAiR team records each person's name, number and the date they agreed before sending anything. The first text identifies VAiR and includes opt-out instructions.

What we send

  • An invitation to request beta access, with a link to vaireferee.com. Usually one message, and no more than 3 per person.
  • No marketing from other companies, and phone numbers are never shared or sold. See our privacy policy and terms.
Sample message
VAiR: Hi Jane! You're invited to beta test VAiR, the AI assistant for soccer referees: instant Laws of the Game rulings and misconduct reports. Request access: https://vaireferee.com/?signup Reply STOP to opt out

Opting in, getting help and opting out

  • Reply START to opt in (or back in) to VAiR texts.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts. You'll get one confirmation and nothing after that.
Reply to START
VAiR: You're subscribed to VAiR beta invitations. Up to 3 msgs total. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
Reply to HELP
VAiR: Beta invitations for VAiR, the AI referee assistant at vaireferee.com. Questions? Email admin@vaireferee.com. Reply STOP to opt out.
Reply to STOP
VAiR: You're unsubscribed and won't receive any more texts. Reply START to resubscribe.

Message frequency varies (at most 3 messages). Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Questions: admin@vaireferee.com.
