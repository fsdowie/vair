VAiR privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

VAiR (vaireferee.com) is an AI assistant for soccer referees, currently in beta. This policy explains what information VAiR collects, how it's used, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a phone number if you choose to give one.
  • What you ask VAiR: your questions, VAiR's answers, and anything you add such as game details or misconduct reports, so the service can work and improve.
  • Basic usage: sign-in times and how many questions you ask.

How we use it

  • To run VAiR: answering your questions, saving your history, and keeping your account secure.
  • To contact you about VAiR: invitations, account emails and, if you've agreed, text messages (see below).
  • To fix problems and improve VAiR's answers.

Your questions are processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate answers. Our hosting and database providers store data on our behalf. They may use it only to provide those services to VAiR.

Text messages and phone numbers

We text only people who gave us their mobile number and agreed to receive VAiR texts. How that works is described on our text messages page.

We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text-messaging opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The only exception is the text-message provider that delivers our messages, which may use them only to deliver VAiR texts.

Reply STOP to any VAiR text to stop receiving them, or HELP for help.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing us.
  • We keep your information while your account is active, and delete it on request.
