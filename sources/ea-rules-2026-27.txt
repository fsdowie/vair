EA TECHNICAL STANDARDS 2026/27 (Elite Academy League)

These standards are reviewed and approved annually by the League Commissioner, Standards Board, and Technical Advisory Group, and guide the overall structure and behavior expected within the league. Everyone who is part of the EA is accountable to these league standards.

--- GENERAL ---

Ages: U11, U12, U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, U19
EA Playoff Ages: U11, U12, U13, U14, U15, U16, U17, U19
High School Participation: All EA Players are permitted to participate in High School Soccer.
College Player Participation: Players who have participated or are participating in any college program at any level (Division I, Junior College, etc.) are NOT permitted to participate in the EA.
Professional Player Participation: Players who have participated or are participating professionally at any level are NOT permitted to participate in EA.
Outside Competition: All EA teams are permitted to participate in outside competition as long as EA league matches are prioritized.
Outside Talent Identification: Players are eligible to participate in approved outside talent identification events with U.S. Soccer, Canadian Soccer, and all FIFA recognized international federations, along with other Talent Identification events.

--- ROSTERS ---

Roster Minimum: Minimum of 11 Players per EA Team (9v9). Minimum of 14 Players per EA Team (11v11).
Roster Maximum: Maximum of 30 Players per EA team at one time.
Matchday Roster: Maximum of 20 Players per EA team.
Overage Players: Maximum of 3 overage Players (born between July 31, 2007 - Aug 1, 2006) on the U19 roster are permitted. Players cannot be registered as Late Developers. Players who have participated or are participating in any college program at any level (Division I, Junior College, etc.) are not permitted to participate in the EA.
Bio Band / Late Developer Players: Maximum of 3 per EA team.
Playing Time: Players are eligible to play in an older age group while being rostered in their younger birth year age group, so long as they meet competition standards and play only 90 minutes per day across a maximum of 2 matches within all competitions.
Roster Adds: EA teams can add an unlimited number of Players during the EA season, maintaining a maximum of 30 total.
Roster Freeze: Rosters will freeze on a designated date prior (May 9th, 2027) to playoffs for eligible teams.
Player Movement: During the season, player movement will not be permitted between clubs within the EA, unless agreed upon by both clubs.
  - Players can only be registered for one club within EA at a time.
  - Consideration may permit movement in certain situations.
  - If a Player is released from their current EA club, that Player can join a different EA club during the season.
  - If a Player is released from their current EA club, that Player cannot play against their previous club.
  - Disputes will be handled by the EA Committee.
  - Player must be in good financial standing with the current club.

--- DESIGNATIONS ---

Player Designations: There will not be any player designations other than Late Developers, Overage Players, and Trapped Players.
Overage Players (O): Maximum of 3 overage players (i.e., birth year of 2006) on the U19 roster are permitted. Players cannot be registered as Late Developers.
Late Developers (L): Three (3) Late Developers can be approved per age group. All age groups are eligible. Must complete Late Developer forms and receive approval from the EA.

--- STAFF ---

Staff Requirements:
  - Academy Director: Each club should have one in place and must have a B License or equivalent.
  - Head Coach must have a C License or equivalent, or provide proof of a coaching license plan to reach the C License.
Matchday Staff:
  - Maximum of 5 coaching staff members permitted in the technical area and must be listed on the Digital Match Roster within Mod 11 (ATC).
  - There must be a minimum of two (2) registered staff members in the technical area for each match.
  - Any staff members working with EA or in the technical area during EA matches must complete the entire registration process via LeagueApps and be on the DMR on Mod 11.

--- COMPETITION ---

Match Timeframe: Players can only play 90 minutes per day across a maximum of 2 matches in any competition, including Outside Competition. Players should not appear in more than 2 consecutive match days during the regular season.
Match Ball: Size 5 match ball must be used for all U14-U19 matches. Size 4 must be used for all U13 matches.
Hydration: Home club must provide adequate hydration for both teams.
Technical Area: Home club must provide adequate seating (benches) for both teams.
Athletic Trainer: Home club must provide an ATC for all matches, available to both home and away Players. If both teams arrive at the field and the Home team does not provide an ATC, the match will not be played, and the home team will receive a forfeit and fine.
Match Video: Home Club must record all matches (U15-U19). The camera must be to a level that records with sound.
Spectators: Home club must ensure there are no spectators/parents in the technical area or on that sideline. Each club is responsible for the behavior of their spectators.

Match Length:
  - U16-U19: 2 x 45 minute halves with 15 minute half time
  - U14-U15: 2 x 40 minute halves with 15 minute half time
  - U13: 2 x 35 minute halves with 10 minute half time
  - U11-U12: 2 x 30 minute halves with 10 minute half time
Warm-Up Periods: Teams must be provided with an appropriate warm-up area 30 minutes prior to kick-off.
Age Group Order: Recommended that older age groups play first to accommodate younger Players 'playing up'.
Kick Off Times: No earlier than 9:00 AM without permission from the EA.
Time between Matches: Minimum of 18 hours between matches if playing on consecutive days, unless approved by the EA.
Matchday Roster: Maximum of 20 Players on matchday rosters.
Substitutions: Matches in the U13-U19 age groups will operate under the FIFA-7 players "per half" substitution system. Once replaced, players cannot re-enter the match during the same half. There shall be a maximum of 3 moments to make nine (9) substitutions per half.
  [Note: the separate EA Referee Handout 2026-2027 states a maximum of 3 moments to make seven (7) substitutions per half or in overtime, for U13-U19 — this conflicts with the 9-substitution figure in the Standards deck above. Flag this discrepancy if asked precisely how many substitutions are allowed, and recommend confirming with EA/the assignor, since the two official documents disagree.]
Match Changes: The EA must approve any request for schedule changes. Any requests for substantial changes in time or location received within two weeks of the match may result in a fine.

--- DISCIPLINE ---

Red Cards: Red Card accumulation follows a player or staff member across all age groups and must be served in the player/staff member's primary age group.
Red Card accumulation for staff:
  - 1st offense = 2 match suspension
  - 2nd offense = subject to additional discipline
  - 3rd offense = subject to additional discipline and potential long-term suspension
Red Cards received in friendly matches will only result in suspension if deemed egregious.

Yellow Cards: Yellow Card accumulation follows a player or staff member across all age groups and must be served in the player/staff member's primary age group. If a player or staff member receives 2 yellow cards in the same match resulting in a red card, only the first yellow card counts towards yellow card accumulation.
Yellow Card Accumulation for players: 5 Yellow Cards (YC) = 1 match; 10 YC = 2 additional matches; 15 YC = 3 additional matches.
Yellow Card Accumulation for staff: 3 Yellow Cards (YC) = 1 match; 5 YC = 2 additional matches; 7 YC = 3 additional matches.

Discipline within Fixture:
  - If a player receives a Yellow Card in two different matches within the same fixture, both cards count towards their Yellow Card accumulation.
  - If a player receives a Red Card in the first match of a split fixture, they will not be able to appear in the rest of the matches in the fixture.
Fixture: When two clubs play against each other across multiple age groups over one weekend, all matches between the two clubs are considered part of the fixture.

--- FACILITY & TRAINING ---

Field Standards for Matches: EA clubs are expected to provide appropriate, high-quality fields to host EA matches.
Minimum Field Sizes:
  - U13 = minimum of 65x84
  - U14 = minimum of 65x96
  - U15-U19 = minimum of 70x105
Any fields outside of these standards must be approved by the EA before the season starts.
Match Locations: All matches played on the same day are expected to be played at the same facility, unless the visiting club agrees to multiple facilities being utilized. EA encourages neutral sites to prevent overnight stays when applicable.
Training: Three (3) training days per week during the regular season.
Rest Day: Individual players must be given at least one day off per week.

EA REFEREE HANDOUT 2026-2027

LAWS OF THE GAME: Unless otherwise provided in these Regulations, all games shall be played in accordance with the Laws of the Game in force at the time of the competition and as laid down by the International Football Association Board. In the case of any discrepancy in the interpretation of the Laws of the Game, the English version shall be authoritative.

MATCH FORMAT:
  - U11, U12 (9v9): Two 30-minute halves with a 10-minute halftime
  - U13: Two 35-minute halves with a 10-minute halftime
  - U14, U15: Two 40-minute halves with a 15-minute halftime
  - U16, U17, U19: Two 45-minute halves with a 15-minute halftime
The home team will provide 3 match balls for each game.

MOD11 / MODULAR11: All Referees receive match access to Modular11 from their assignments in Assignr.com. The home team is responsible for ensuring Referees have access to Modular11 if there is a late assignment.
Player Check-In: Players & Staff must be viewable in the MOD11 Match Details page.
Match Reporting: Referees must submit the Match Report immediately following the match, or at the very least on the same day the match was played.

ROSTER RULES:
  - Game Day Roster for U13-U19 is 18 players, and for U11-U12 it is 14 players (MOD11 will not allow teams to add more).
  - A player may only play 90 total minutes per day.

SUBBING RULES:
  - U11-U12: Unlimited substitutions with re-entry.
  - U13-U19: operates under the FIFA-7 players "per half" substitution system. Once replaced, players cannot re-enter the game during that half of play. There shall be a maximum of 3 moments to make seven (7) substitutions in any half or in overtime of any Elite Academy League competition.
  - Substitution passes are required for U13-U19 age groups with each substitution except at halftime. A player who re-enters improperly should be noted in the match report in MOD11, and league staff will sanction the team accordingly.

HEAD INJURIES:
  - Any player suspected of suffering a head injury may be substituted for evaluation without the substitution counting against the team's total number of allowed substitutions during the game.
  - If the club has used all their subs and/or moments when a head injury occurs, they will still be allowed to make a temporary substitute, bringing on a player that was previously subbed off.
  - If the player is deemed by the HCP (Health Care Professional) or ATC not to have suffered a concussion, the player may re-enter the game at any stoppage of play and must replace the original substitute; this head injury evaluation substitution will not count as a used substitution.
  - The temporary substitute will be able to re-enter the game at a later time, if they had not previously played in that half.
  - If the player is not cleared to return to play by the HCP or ATC, the replacement player will no longer be considered a temporary substitution (i.e. it counts as a normal, permanent substitution).
